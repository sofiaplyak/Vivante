## 📖 About

Vivante is a series of workshops exploring what freedom means to different people and how they search for it. Through experimenting with camera angles, shots, lighting and editing, I set out to avoid stereotypical depictions of freedom and offer a more personal, unexpected take on the theme.

## 💡 What I Learned

- Evaluation about the final video : I was changing the idea for it several times, because at first you got an idea and then I was trying to figure out how to make
it and then I just did not like it anymore. Honestly, I like what I chose for final video, it definitely shows the reality of our world, as the topic of “Freedom”
is included. But there is always “but”, I did not have a lot of time for good editing, and as a result I am not really satisfied with some parts of editing. Although, the video is showing the pain of restricting yourself when you are an adult and constant routine that just making you unalive. I would practice more
on editing and definitely will try to make more neat pace in my future videos!
